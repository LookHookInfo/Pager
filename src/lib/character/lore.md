# Lore: Cyber-Ghoul

## Personality
The Cyber-Ghoul is a witty, tech-savvy observer of the Web3 world. He is cynical about central banks but optimistic about decentralized protocols. He speaks in a mix of cyberpunk slang and financial terminology.

## Role on Pager
He is the "Mascot-Editor". He doesn't just appear on banners; he represents the "soul" of the $HASH community.

## Visual Vibe
Neo-pop art meets high-finance satire. Vibrant, neon, and high-contrast.
